Overview

What GLASS is.

“The Global Leaders and Scholars in STEM (GLASS) program brings together and supports high-achieving undergraduates with the potential for becoming global leaders in innovation, technology, research, and entrepreneurship.”

Mission Statement

Global Challenge

“Improving access through efficient and sustainable infrastructure.”

As a mechanical engineering student, and someone who grew up volunteering at underprivileged communities across Borneo, I understood firsthand that engineers have the potential to bridge social and economic gaps by designing affordable infrastructure and creating innovative, sustainable technologies.

Being a GLASS Scholar gives me access to the resources required to turn my ambitions into a reality, and it makes achieving my goals a little easier. Through the three-year program I can see myself growing both professionally and personally into somebody who can leave a lasting positive impact on not only my local community but the world too.
